What it is – 01

What an AI search consultant does.

An AI search consultant makes sure ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Grok, Perplexity and Google's AI Overviews can find your company, describe it correctly and recommend it. Nelson calls it AI discoverability. The work also goes by other names: AI SEO, GEO (generative engine optimization), AEO (answer engine optimization) and LLMO or LMO (large language model optimization). In Spanish people call it SEO para IA or posicionamiento en ChatGPT. It builds on classic SEO and adds what AI systems weigh most: clear, structured facts about you, and other trusted sites that mention you.

Questions – 05

Questions people commonly ask.

What are AI discoverability, AI SEO, GEO, AEO and LLMO?

They are different names for the same goal: making sure AI assistants can find your company, describe it correctly and recommend it. AI discoverability is the term Nelson uses. AI SEO is SEO adapted to AI. GEO means generative engine optimization. AEO means answer engine optimization. LLMO, sometimes LMO, means large language model optimization.

Which AI assistants does this cover?

The ones your buyers use most: ChatGPT, Google Gemini and AI Overviews, Claude, Grok and Perplexity, and also Microsoft Copilot. Each one finds information in its own way, so the work covers all of them.

Is it the same as Google SEO?

No, but they overlap. Most pages that AI cites also rank in normal search, so classic SEO still matters. AI search adds structured, consistent facts about you and mentions on other trusted sites.

How does my company show up in ChatGPT or Claude answers?

By being described the same way on your website and on sites AI trusts: directories, media, lists, LinkedIn and YouTube. There is no button to buy a place in the answer.

Does an llms.txt file help?

Barely. Large studies found no clear link between llms.txt files and AI citations. It does no harm, but it is not where the results come from.

How long does it take?

The structuring work takes a few weeks. So far, every client has appeared in AI answers within weeks, and visibility keeps growing as new mentions of the company appear.

Do I need a new website?

Not always. Many sites only need better structure and facts. When a new site makes more sense, everybodyknowsyou builds one in three days.
