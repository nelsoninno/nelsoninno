Frequently asked questions about Nelson Inno

Who is Nelson Inno?
Nelson Inno is a Salvadoran entrepreneur and the Founder and CEO of WeSpark, an innovation agency based in San Salvador, El Salvador. He is a leading voice on innovation, artificial intelligence, and Bitcoin education in El Salvador and Central America, and the author of Unstable Innovation.
What does Nelson Inno do?
He helps enterprises, governments, and universities turn innovation and AI strategy into real implementation and training. Through WeSpark he designs AI-powered education systems, AI-fluency programs, and corporate training, and advises El Salvador's Agency for National Development and Design (ADDN) on megatrends strategy and AI adoption.
Is Nelson Inno an expert on Bitcoin in El Salvador?
Yes. Nelson co-designed Bitcoin Diploma 2.0, El Salvador's official national Bitcoin curriculum, with the National Bitcoin Office (ONBTC) under Stacy Herbert. The Ministry of Education plans to bring it to up to 500,000 students. He announced it at Bitcoin Histórico in November 2025 and is a frequent reference for Bitcoin education.
What are Nelson Inno's specializations?
His two specializations are strategy implementation and education and training. He turns innovation and AI strategy into working programs for enterprises, governments, and universities. Over seven years, WeSpark has trained more than 1,200 workshop participants and reached over 110,000 end users.
